# Dalata Hotel Group — Digital Transformation Business Case

An independent, self-directed digital transformation business case built for Dalata Hotel Group (`dalatahotelgroup.com`), Ireland's largest hotel operator.

- **Author:** Valeriia Slastin
- **Type:** Independent portfolio project — not affiliated with, commissioned by, or reviewed by Dalata Hotel Group, EY, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, or any professional services firm.
- **Primary deliverable:** Excel workbook (`Dalata_Digital_Transformation_Business_Case.xlsx`)

## About the Project

This case study evaluates Dalata Hotel Group's public digital transformation footprint — website/CRM investment, brand refresh, and booking channel strategy — using a standard consulting business-case framework: digital maturity scoring, competitive benchmarking, an Impact vs. Effort prioritization matrix, and a formula-driven financial model.

All company facts are sourced from public information (see the workbook's **Company & Public Context** tab for full citations). The financial model uses Dalata's real, published 2024 revenue as an anchor but models booking-channel mix using general hospitality industry benchmarks, not confirmed internal Dalata data — this is explicitly flagged throughout the workbook.

## What's Inside

1. **README** — project scope and disclaimers
2. **Company & Public Context** — sourced facts, each with a citation
3. **Digital Maturity Assessment** — scored across 5 standard consulting categories
4. **Competitive Benchmark** — comparison against two other hotel operators
5. **Impact vs Effort Matrix** — prioritized initiatives, formula-classified into Quick Win / Major Project / Fill-In / Reconsider
6. **Business Case** — formula-driven, clearly-labeled illustrative financial model
7. **Implementation Roadmap** — phased (Now / Next / Ongoing) recommendations
8. **Methodology & Caveats** — sources, confidence levels, and modeling assumptions

## Why This Format

Built deliberately without any single firm's internal terminology — the structure (situation → diagnosis → prioritization → business case → roadmap) reflects the general shape of a business case used across audit, advisory, and consulting practices broadly.

## Data Honesty Note

Every figure in this workbook is either directly sourced (with citation) or clearly labeled as a modeled assumption. No claim is made that this represents Dalata Hotel Group's actual internal data, strategy, or results.
